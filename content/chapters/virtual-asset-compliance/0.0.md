# Virtual Asset Compliance — Study Guide

This guide is the course material for the Certified Virtual Asset Compliance Analyst credential. It is educational material only — not legal advice — and it does not state the law of any jurisdiction as it applies to any particular institution or transaction. Virtual-asset regulation changes quickly; MiCA's application dates, FinCEN's rulemaking and national frameworks are all moving targets. Verify current requirements against the regulator and your own compliance policies before relying on anything here.

## Who this guide is for

- **Crypto compliance officers** at exchanges, custodians and wallet providers who own the AML programme day to day.
- **VASP onboarding and monitoring analysts** who need to understand what they are actually looking at on a blockchain explorer.
- **Traditional AML analysts** moving into virtual assets, for whom nearly every instinct has to be re-taught.
- **Fintech and payments risk teams** whose products now touch stablecoins or crypto on-ramps whether they planned to or not.

## Why this subject exists separately

Anti-money laundering for virtual assets is not a chapter in the banking AML manual with the word "crypto" substituted in. The asset moves differently, the evidence is public rather than private, the counterparty is often an address rather than a person, and the jurisdiction that matters may be nowhere at all. A compliance function that treats a Bitcoin transaction like a wire transfer will miss most of what there is to see — and will drown in false positives trying.

The guide therefore starts with what the technology actually is, moves through how value moves and how analysts trace it, and only then covers what a regulated firm must do about it.

## How to use it

Modules build on each other. Module 1 gives you the perimeter — who is regulated and why. Module 2 explains the machinery: keys, transactions, mixers. Module 3 covers the analytical craft of tracing. Module 4 is the operational core — what your institution actually does. Module 5 covers investigations and where the field is heading.

## The one idea to hold onto

On a public blockchain, **the transaction is never the whole story**. An exchange withdrawal of 2 BTC is just two addresses and an amount — the meaning lives in what those addresses have done before and after. Every technique in this guide is a way of recovering that meaning from public data, and every failure mode is a way the meaning gets deliberately obscured.
