Institutional Responsibilities and Data-Driven Detection 1. Governance and Culture Senior management must treat human-trafficking risk as a strategic compliance priority, not a niche CSR topic. Boards should: • Integrate trafficking risk into enterprise AML frameworks. • Allocate resources for TM model calibration specific to exploitation typologies. • Promote cross-functional coordination among AML, fraud, and sanctions teams. 2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) CDD procedures should flag sectors with higher vulnerability — recruitment, hospitality, entertainment, agriculture, and cross-border remittance services. Institutions should: • Obtain business-model justification for cash-intensive clients. • Verify labor-supply chains and subcontractors. • Apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) where migrant-worker or cash-transfer exposure is significant.
