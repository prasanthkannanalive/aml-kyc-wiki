Governance & Ownership: Turning Awareness into Accountable Controls Objective: Define who owns human-trafficking (HT) risk across the three lines of defense, and how it is embedded from Board to analysts. 1.1 Governance Model (Three Lines) • Line 1 (Business & Ops): Own HT risk in day-to-day onboarding, servicing, payments, and client lifecycle. Maintain playbooks for high-risk sectors (recruitment agencies, labor contractors, hospitality, logistics, entertainment, cash-intensive SMEs, high-risk remitters). • Line 2 (Compliance/MLRO/TM/KYC/Sanctions): Set HT policies, run EWRA, calibrate TM/KYT rules, approve EDD, control quality of STR/SAR narratives, manage PPP/NGO/FIU engagements. • Line 3 (Internal Audit): Independently test design & operating effectiveness of HT controls; validate HT-specific KRIs/KPIs; evaluate STR quality, timeliness, and rationale. 1.2 Board & Executive Oversight • Board Risk Committee: Receives quarterly HT risk dashboards (alerts, escalations, STRs, law-enforcement referrals, de-risking decisions). • Senior Management: Signs off on HT risk appetite, approves resources for AI analytics, blockchain forensics, and staff training. 1.3 Roles & RACI (example) • Policy Owner (Head of FCC): Accountable for HT Policy & Standards. • MLRO: Accountable for STR filing & FIU interface. • TM Lead: Responsible for scenario design, thresholds, model performance. • KYC Lead: Responsible for sectoral EDD, UBO validation, OSINT. • Sanctions Lead: Consulted on forced-labor, human-rights sanctions risks. • Ops/Branches: Responsible to execute front-line checks; raise Red Flag forms. • Audit: Informed/Independent testing. 1.4 Policies to Formalize (minimum set) • Human Trafficking & Exploitation Risk Standard (scope, definitions, red flags). • Sector EDD Standard (recruiters, labor brokers, entertainment/hospitality, logistics). • TM/KYT Scenario Standard (rule IDs HTRAF-01…20; periodic calibration calendar). • STR Governance Standard (writing guide, timelines, MLRO approvals). • Intelligence Sharing & PPP Protocol (confidentiality, data-privacy safeguards).
