Risk Assessment & Control Library (Practical, Deployable) Objective: Embed HT risk in the Enterprise-Wide Risk Assessment (EWRA) and map it to an actionable control library that regulators and auditors can trace. 2.1 EWRA: How to Score HT Risk • Risk Drivers: Products (remittances, payroll, prepaid, e-money, P2P, crypto on/off-ramp), Customers (labor recruiters, cash-intensive SMEs, NGOs with ambiguous scope), Geography (transit/source/ destination corridors), Channels (non-face-to-face, agent networks, DEX/P2P), Delivery (outsourced KYC, correspondent banking, nested VASP relationships). • Scoring: Use weighted factors (0–5) with multiplier for known HT corridors and adverse media. • Data Sources: Internal alert stats, FIU feedback letters, PPP bulletins, NGO inputs, adverse media, sanctions/HR designations. 2.2 Control Library (sample structure) Onboarding / KYC • UBO verification with enhanced documentary/OSINT checks for recruiters & labor suppliers. • Mandatory business model justification for cash-intensive clients; site-visit or video-verification where feasible. • Adverse media screening keywords (e.g., “debt bondage,” “placement fee,” “recruitment scam”). EDD Triggers • High-risk sectors; negative NGO reports; linkages to sanctioned/HR-flagged suppliers; multiple accounts sharing IP/addresses. TM/KYT Scenarios (rule IDs) • HTRAF-01: Multiple low-value remittances from unrelated senders → single beneficiary in high-risk corridor. • HTRAF-02: Immediate cash withdrawal of full payroll deposits across a cluster of accounts. • HTRAF-03: Payments labeled “visa/training/placement fees” from diverse senders. • HTRAF-04: Crypto micro-payments → ad platforms/ad credit merchants tied to adult services. • HTRAF-05: Fiat↔crypto↔gift-card chains; mixer or privacy-coin hops. • HTRAF-06: Recurrent identical deposits on Fridays from 1 foreign sponsor → multiple “manpower” shells.
