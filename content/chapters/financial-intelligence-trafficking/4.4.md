# Training & Competency: Role-Based Model (accreditation-aligned)

Objective: Build a durable, role-based learning pathway so every function can detect and escalate trafficking risk consistently. 4.1 Role Profiles & Learning Objectives
- Frontline / Branch / Ops: Spot behavioral red flags, complete Red-Flag Forms, trigger immediate escalation. Learning: HT basics, interview cues, red-flag checklist, documentary anomalies.
- KYC / Onboarding: Validate UBO, sector risk, document authenticity, corridor risk, adverse media. Learning: Sector EDD playbooks (recruiters, labor brokers, hospitality), OSINT, UBO tracing.
- TM / Investigations (Banking & VASP): Tune HTRAF rules, link analysis, cross-channel patterns, case documentation. Learning: Scenario testing, graph analytics, KYT, crypto typologies, narrative drafting.
- Sanctions / Trade / Supply Chain: Forced-labor exposure, HR sanctions regimes, supplier EDD, dual-use & customs signals. Learning: Screening deep-dives, supplier attestation review, trade-finance flags.
- MLRO / FCC Leadership: Governance, STR quality oversight, PPP engagement, model risk, audit readiness. Learning: FIU liaison, board reporting, calibration governance, evidence packs.
- Internal Audit / Model Risk: Design & operating effectiveness testing; validation of alerts, coverage, and KPIs. Learning: Test scripts, sampling frameworks, challenger models, documentation standards. 4.2 Curriculum & Hours (reference design)
- Foundation (All Staff): 60–90 mins e-learning | annual refresher.
- Practitioner (KYC/TM/Sanctions/Trade): 4–6 hrs role-specific + 1 lab (case file & STR).
- Advanced (Investigations/MLRO): 8–12 hrs workshops + tabletop exercise with PPP/FIU.
- Crypto/VASP Add-On (where relevant): 3–4 hrs KYT + blockchain forensics lab. 4.3 Verification & Credentials (accreditation-aligned)
- Maintain a Competency Matrix mapping roles → skills → courses → assessment.
- Require a capstone case (end-to-end alert → investigation memo → STR narrative).
- Encourage staff to stack recognized, accreditation-compliant certifications (see “Certification Ecosystem Note” below) alongside this FIHT credential for a complete capability stack.
