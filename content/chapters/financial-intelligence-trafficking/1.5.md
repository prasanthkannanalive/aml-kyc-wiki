Global Regulatory and Institutional Frameworks Combatting human trafficking through financial intelligence requires a harmonized regulatory response. While each jurisdiction varies, core principles derive from FATF Recommendations and international conventions. 1. FATF Standards • Recommendation 1 (Risk-Based Approach): Institutions must assess and mitigate ML/TF risks — explicitly including trafficking as a predicate offense. • Recommendation 10 (Customer Due Diligence): Mandates identification of beneficial owners to prevent use of front companies or agents. • Recommendation 15 (New Technologies): Applies AML obligations to virtual assets and VASPs. • Recommendation 20 (Suspicious Transaction Reporting): Requires immediate reporting of suspicious activity linked to predicate crimes.
