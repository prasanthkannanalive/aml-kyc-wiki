International Cooperation and Information Exchange Mechanisms Global trafficking networks operate across multiple borders — often exploiting data silos between jurisdictions. International cooperation is therefore central to disrupting trafficking-linked money flows. 1. FIU-to-FIU Collaboration Through the Egmont Group, FIUs can request intelligence, trace asset movement, and coordinate investigations. For compliance teams, this means: • STRs must contain clear identifiers (names, addresses, wallet IDs, phone numbers) to facilitate matching across jurisdictions. • Use of standardized STR formats (e.g., goAML XML schema) helps data-sharing efficiency. • Cooperation requests are often triggered by well-documented STRs filed by banks and fintechs. 2.
