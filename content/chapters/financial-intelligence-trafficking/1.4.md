Typologies and Financial Flow Models Trafficking networks mirror corporate supply chains — organized, multi-layered, and internationally connected. Recognizing their financial flow models enables compliance professionals to map how illicit funds enter and circulate through the legitimate economy. 1. Placement and Initial Funding Criminals inject cash derived from exploitation (wages withheld, illicit payments, or ransoms) into the system via: • Small, repetitive deposits across multiple accounts. • Currency exchange or remittance operators in high-migrant regions. • Payments to “labor agencies” or “training centers” that exist only on paper. 2.
