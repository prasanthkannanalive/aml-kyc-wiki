The Role of Financial Systems in Enabling and Detecting Trafficking Financial systems are both an enabler and a solution in the fight against trafficking. Without access to financial infrastructure, traffickers cannot store, move, or enjoy their profits. Yet, the same systems that facilitate exploitation also possess the data, technology, and intelligence to expose it. How Financial Systems Are Exploited Traffickers leverage gaps in compliance oversight: • Cash-heavy operations: Use of deposits below threshold limits (structuring). • Third-party accounts: Victims’ wages directed into accounts controlled by others. • Cross-border layering: Moving funds between multiple countries with inconsistent AML enforcement.
